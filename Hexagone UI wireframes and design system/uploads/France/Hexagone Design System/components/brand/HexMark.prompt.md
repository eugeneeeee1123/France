The hexagon "H" monogram — Hexagone's one signature mark; use in the nav, footer and once as a hero crest. One hexagon moment per page beyond chrome.

```jsx
<HexMark size={32} withWordmark />
<HexMark size={24} tone="paper" withWordmark />
<HexMark size={100} filled />
```

Never tile it as a background pattern. Gold on navy in the masthead, paper in the footer.
